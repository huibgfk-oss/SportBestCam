SPORTBESTCAM 0002.10.3 — AUDIO FX REAL FIX

Problem confirmed in current main:
SportEffectsState queued SFX into SportEffectsAudioMixer, but
GLRecordingPipeline did not call mixIntoPcm16() at all before AAC.

Fix:
1. Inserts exactly one SportEffectsAudioMixer.mixIntoPcm16() call after the
   audioMuted block and before `int offset = 0` / AAC encoder input.
2. SportEffectsAudioMixer.trigger() now also plays a local AudioTrack monitor.
   Therefore the operator hears the SFX immediately when tapping it.
3. The same SFX remains queued for digital PCM mixing into the recorded MP4.
4. Monitor playback is lower than recorded SFX to reduce microphone re-capture.
5. Adds Logcat diagnostics:
   - SFX monitor PLAY
   - SFX RECORDER PCM
6. Debug versionNameSuffix -> beta10.9.

Important:
If recording audio is disabled completely, there is no AAC track to mix into.
The local monitor will still be audible.
