SportBestCam Build 0002.5.4 Preview / Teams UX

- Adds hidden Camera Tools option:
  "Fundal umplere preview: ON/OFF".
- ON keeps the historical zoom-fill + dim background behind the sharp camera frame.
- OFF removes the duplicated enlarged/dimmed background and uses clean black
  letterbox/pillarbox bars.
- The setting affects LIVE PREVIEW ONLY. Recorded video is never changed.
- Default is ON to preserve current behaviour after update.

Teams dialog:
- Replaces the confusing 4 edit fields with only 2:
  "Nume Echipa A" and "Nume Echipa B".
- Short/compact team labels are generated automatically.
- Landscape shows the two team fields side by side.
- Portrait shows them vertically.
- Existing team/scoring/video metadata functionality is preserved.

All Build 0002.5.3a fixes and previous compatibility/rebrand fixes remain unchanged.
