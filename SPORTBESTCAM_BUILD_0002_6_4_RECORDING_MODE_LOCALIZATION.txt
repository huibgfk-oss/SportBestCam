SportBestCam Build 0002.6.4 - Recording Mode & Localization

Recording profiles
- Adds Match recording and Simple video modes.
- Match recording keeps SportBestCam match HUD, score, teams, match timer and Auto Goal.
- Simple video hides match HUD/teams/score/match timer/Auto Goal from preview and fullscreen.
- Simple video does not burn match information into the saved video.
- The normal SportBestCam watermark/logo continues to follow the existing watermark setting.
- Recording profile cannot be changed while a recording is already active, preventing mixed-content files.

Video filenames
- Match recordings use descriptive filenames:
  SportBestCam_<sport-or-title>_<Team-A>_vs_<Team-B>_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.mp4
- Simple video uses the standard filename:
  SportBestCam_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.mp4
- Team/title filename parts are sanitized and length-limited.
- Split recordings keep _partN suffixes.
- Stream_ output keeps its existing naming contract.
- Records timestamp parsing now accepts:
  SportBestCam_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.mp4
  SportBestCam_<title>_<A>_vs_<B>_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.mp4
  SportBestCam_<title>_<A>_vs_<B>_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss_partN.mp4
  legacy FadCam_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.mp4
  so sorting, NEW badges and date grouping continue to work.

Localization
- New SportBestCam UI strings use English as the default resource language.
- The SportBestCam additions are translated for every language already exposed by the app:
  Chinese, Arabic, French, Turkish, Pashto, Indonesian, Italian, Greek,
  German, Spanish, Estonian and Russian.
- Romanian is added to the language selector and has dedicated values-ro SportBestCam resources.
- Internal preference keys and technical identifiers remain language-neutral.

Compatibility
- Camera, Media3, recording pipeline, pause/resume, storage, FadRec, streaming,
  Remote, gallery and legacy FadCam storage compatibility are not removed.
- Build includes all UI fixes through 0002.6.3b.
